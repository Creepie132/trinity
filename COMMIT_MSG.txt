fix(mobile-header): swap Back and Burger positions for correct RTL

Problem: On Hebrew (RTL) the burger icon appeared on the left and the
back arrow on the right — reversed from what's natural for an RTL reader.

Root cause: DOM order was [Back | Logo | Burger]. Flexbox `justify-between`
does mirror items in an RTL direction context, so in RTL this rendered as
[Burger-left | Logo-center | Back-right]. That's the wrong placement for
Hebrew: an RTL user expects the menu on the right (start of text flow)
and the back arrow on the left (end of flow, pointing forward-in-text).

Fix: swap the DOM order to [Burger | Logo | Back] in both mobile headers.
Result:
  - RTL (he): Burger right, Back left  (natural for Hebrew)
  - LTR (ru): Burger left,  Back right (natural for Russian)

All achieved through native flexbox mirroring + `dir` on <html>. No JS
conditions, no hardcoded direction checks.
