fix(callback): superadmin with own org respects landing preference

Problem: Superadmin users (admin_users) who also own an organization
(org_users owner/staff role) were always redirected to /dashboard after
Google OAuth login, ignoring their saved default_landing_page preference.

Root cause: The callback logic treated ALL admin_users (except sales_agents)
as pure system admins and hardcoded /dashboard. But most superadmins in
Trinity are also business owners (e.g. Vlad = Amber Solutions superadmin
+ owner of Amber Solutions org) and should behave like regular users
regarding the landing-page preference.

Fix: If adminRecord is NOT a sales_agent AND user has a row in org_users,
read default_landing_page from user_nav_preferences and redirect there.
Pure admin accounts (no org membership) keep the /dashboard hardcode.
